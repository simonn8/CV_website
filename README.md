# Simon Nachtergaele — Portfolio, Sound Archives & Selected Library

Contemporary personal portfolio, professional engineering dossier, DJ broadcast archives, and curated 47-book library.

Designed with inspiration from **Horst Arts & Music** and **Ongehoord** visual identity.

---

## Sections

- **01 // About & Dossier**: Background in Business Engineering (Major in Data Science) at KU Leuven, IMT Business School Paris, Universidad de Granada, and Sirris QRM Silver certification.
- **02 // Experience & Systems**: Detailed career history covering supply chain planning at Sports & Leisure Group NV (€2M EBITDA unit, real-time stock visibility platform, Pipedrive CRM automation) and 24Flow (MES software, Salesforce platform).
- **03 // Sound & Broadcasts**: Embedded video/audio player streaming 5 recorded sets from Radio Ruit, Comala Radio, and YAMI RECORDS.
- **04 // Selected Library**: 47-volume digital library synced with Goodreads, featuring instant title/author/keyword search, shelf filters, and sorting.
- **05 // Contact & Frequencies**: Direct inquiry channels, LinkedIn, GitHub, Goodreads, and Instagram (@ongehoord.events).
